Questions companies ask.

We already have LinkedIn Learning / Coursera. Why this?

Keep it. A catalog is for people who already know what to look for. Lumicamp is the structured on-ramp for the 80% who don't, and it hands them back to your catalog with confidence.

Does this make us compliant with the EU AI Act?

No product can guarantee that. Lumicamp gives you role-appropriate training, evidence of completion, and a mapping to literacy competencies — the documentation a regulator asks for. Your counsel decides what else your program needs.

Our people are too busy for training.

That's why it's ten minutes a day on a phone, not a two-day workshop. In the pilot we measure active days per learner — if people don't use it, you don't pay.

Do you support SSO or our LMS?

On the roadmap and built to order for Enterprise contracts. For a pilot, invite links and a CSV export are enough — let's prove the learning outcome first.
