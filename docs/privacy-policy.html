AI Fluent — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-19  |  Last updated: 2026-05-19

AI Fluent is a mobile learning app that helps you understand artificial intelligence through guided lessons, daily news, and hands-on tools. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the third-party services involved.

1. What We Collect

  • Email address — required to create an account. Provided by you at sign-up and stored in Supabase Auth.
  • Display name — the name you choose during onboarding, stored in your profile.
  • Learning data — lessons completed, streak history, and achievement badges earned. Stored in our database to track your progress.
  • AI tool inputs — text you type in AI Tools (Email Writer, Prompt Builder, and other guided workflows) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate responses. We do not store these inputs in our systems.

2. How We Use It

  • To create and authenticate your account
  • To save and display your learning progress across sessions and devices
  • To power AI-generated responses in the app's interactive tools

We do not use your data to build advertising profiles, run experiments, or for any purpose beyond operating the app as described.

3. Third-Party Services

AI Fluent uses the following third-party services. By using the app, your data may be processed by these providers as described below:

  • Supabase (supabase.com) — handles account authentication and stores your profile and learning progress data in a hosted database. Supabase servers are located in the EU (by default). Supabase enforces encryption at rest and in transit.
  • Anthropic (anthropic.com) — provides the Claude AI API that powers AI Tools. Text you enter in AI Tools is sent to Anthropic's API for processing. Anthropic does not use API data for model training. Per Anthropic's standard policy, API inputs and outputs may be retained for up to 7 days for security purposes, then deleted.

4. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your data to any third parties
  • We do not use advertising networks or advertising identifiers (no ad SDKs are integrated)
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or similar services)
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites

5. Data Retention

Your account data — email address, display name, lesson progress, streak history, and achievements — is retained for as long as your account exists. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email below.

Text inputs sent to Anthropic's Claude API during AI Tools sessions are subject to Anthropic's 7-day retention policy and are not stored by AI Fluent.

6. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Supabase enforces encryption at rest for all stored data. We do not store passwords — authentication is handled securely by Supabase Auth.
